# Game Design — Crossword Master

## Identity
- Genre: Kelime ve Bilgi
- Platforms: iOS, Android; portrait or landscape chosen per mechanics
- Engine: Flutter + Flame
- Audience: casual players initially; validate with user research

## Gameplay loop
Launch > mode selection > tutorial > round/level > results > rewards > progress > next round.
The exact rules, controls, camera, failure and victory conditions are design tasks,
not invented competitor facts.

## Proposed systems
Modes: classic campaign, endless/score attack, daily challenge, practice,
time attack, special events. Apply ONLY modes that make sense for this title.
Systems: settings, unlocks, difficulty, cosmetics, analytics, achievements.
A level editor and data-driven level files are recommended.

## Accessibility
Color-independent signals, adjustable effects, reduced motion,
legible touch targets, clear error/retry paths.

## Intellectual property
Do not copy third-party art, brand marks, audio, levels or proprietary code.
