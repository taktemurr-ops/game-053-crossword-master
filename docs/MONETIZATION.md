# Monetization — Crossword Master

- Opt-in rewarded video for an appropriate gameplay benefit.
- Carefully frequency-capped interstitial ads BETWEEN sessions, never in action.
- In-app purchases: optional cosmetic or booster bundles where appropriate.
- Purchase restoration, age-appropriate ads/consent and disclosure.
- Track retention, session frequency, ARPDAU, eCPM and payer conversion.
- No revenue projections without explicit traffic, geographies and assumptions.
